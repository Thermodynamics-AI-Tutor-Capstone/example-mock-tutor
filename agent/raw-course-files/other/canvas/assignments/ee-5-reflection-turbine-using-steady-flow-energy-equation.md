# EE 5 Reflection - Turbine using Steady Flow Energy Equation

Canvas assignment. Points: 100.0. Due (Summer 2023): None. Submission: online_upload.

Watch the video of Dr. O’Connor doing Explained Example 5. As you watch, compare her explanation of the problem to the way that you and your partner explained the problem to each other in terms of accuracy, depth, and your understanding of the concepts in the problem.

Write a reflection on your understanding by thinking about the following questions. Use the skills you learned in the Study Smarter Session as you're doing this exercise.

- How did your problem solving steps compare to Dr. O’Connor’s explanation?

- Beyond comparing the accuracy of individual steps, how deep was your conceptual understanding? Describe the steps you understood well and areas where your (or your partner's) explanations showed incomplete understanding. 

- Based on your answers to the previous two questions, what do you need to do to improve your understanding and explanation of this material? 

Submit your reflection online, typed on one page, single-spaced, 12-point font. This is an individual assignment - each partner submits a separate reflection.
