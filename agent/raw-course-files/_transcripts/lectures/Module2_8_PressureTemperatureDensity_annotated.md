## Page 2
Mass, moles, and volume

[handwritten] Mass: how much material ($M$) – kg

[handwritten] Moles: quantity of molecules ($N$) → 1 mole = $6.02214199 \times 10^{23}$ part.

[handwritten] Volume: amount of space occupied ($V$, script V = total volume) – m$^3$

## Page 3
Density and specific volume

[handwritten] $$\rho = \frac{M}{V} \sim \left[\frac{\text{kg}}{\text{m}^3}\right]$$

[handwritten] → continuum: $\Delta V = \Delta x \Delta y \Delta z$

[figure] Small cube (fluid element) with an arrow from the text "define $\rho$ at a point"; beside it "$\Delta x, \Delta y, \Delta z \to 0$"; arrow from the cube to "large number of particles inside" (underlined).

[handwritten] → Knudsen number $= \lambda / L$ → $\lambda$ = mean free path, $L$ = dimension of system; $L \gg\gg \lambda$

[handwritten] → mass-specific volume: $$v = \frac{V}{M} = \frac{1}{\rho} \sim \left[\frac{\text{m}^3}{\text{kg}}\right]$$

[handwritten] molar-specific volume ⇒ $$\bar{v} = \frac{V}{N} \sim \left[\frac{\text{m}^3}{\text{mol}}\right]$$

## Page 4
Pressure

[handwritten] → normal force exerted by fluid on an area $\quad P = \frac{F}{A}$

[handwritten] → intensive: $$P = \lim_{\Delta A \to 0} \frac{F_{\text{normal}}}{\Delta A}$$ (the "$\Delta A \to 0$" is double-underlined)

[handwritten] → [figure: small circle with an arrow through it] flux of molecules $\Delta A$
