Steady flow energy equation

[handwritten] $\dot{m}\left(\Delta h + \Delta ke + \Delta \cancel{pe}^{\,0}\right) = \cancel{\dot{Q}}^{\,0} - \dot{W}^{\,0}$

[handwritten] $\Rightarrow \Delta h + \Delta ke = 0$

[handwritten] $(h_2 - h_1) + \frac{1}{2}\left(V_2^{\,2} - V_1^{\,2}\right) = 0$
