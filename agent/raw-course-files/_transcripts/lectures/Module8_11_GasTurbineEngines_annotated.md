## Page 6
Cycle analysis

[figure] Hand sketch of a jet engine as a series of components with station numbers: inlet (1 → 2), compressor (2 → 3), combustor (3 → 4, drawn with two can shapes), turbine (4 → 5), nozzle (5 → 6).

[handwritten] Labels under the stations:
- 1–2: inlet diffuser, $v$ ↓ (velocity decreases)
- 2–3: fan + compressor, $h$ ↑ (the bracket line runs through "fan +"; it may or may not be a strike-through [?])
- 3–4: combustor, heat in
- 4–5: turbine, work out – drive compressor + fan
- 5–6: nozzle

$$\text{OPR} = \frac{P_3}{P_1}$$

[figure] P–V diagram (P vertical, script V = total volume horizontal): state 1 at low pressure; 1→2 short compression rise to state 2; 2→3 compression curve up to state 3 at high P; 3→4 horizontal constant-pressure line to state 4; 4→5 expansion curve down to state 5; 5→6 further expansion to state 6 at the lowest pressure; 6→1 horizontal line back to state 1. Arrows show direction 1→2→3→4→5→6→1.
