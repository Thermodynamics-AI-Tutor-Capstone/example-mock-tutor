## Page 4
Learning Objectives

- Obtain thermodynamic data necessary to solve thermodynamic problems and when necessary use appropriate approximations. These skills include the use of equations of state and/or tabulated property tables.
- Write the First Law of Thermodynamics in their appropriate forms for both closed system and control volume problem.
- Solve problems requiring First Law analysis that produce a simple single answer.
- Make appropriate assumptions when applying the First Law to a "real-world" problem.
- Write the Second Law of Thermodynamics in their appropriate forms for both closed system and control volume problem.
- Apply the Second Law to determine the performance limitations of a given thermodynamic system.
- Apply thermodynamic concepts to describe the performance of the individual components of an engineering system, e.g. a power plant, a jet engine, etc., and then relate that information to the overall performance of the entire system.

Assessments

[figure] Four-column organizational chart:
- **Homework (25%)**: Purpose: Instruction; 10 homeworks, due on Fridays; Promotes both conceptual and quantitative learning; Problems will be similar to exam problems
- **Explained Examples (15%)**: Purpose: Instruction; Due three times per semester; Complete with a partner
