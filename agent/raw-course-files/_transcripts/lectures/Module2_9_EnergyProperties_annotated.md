## Page 5
Enthalpy

[handwritten]
$$H = U + PV \sim [\text{J}]$$
$$h = u + Pv \sim [\text{J/kg}]$$
$$\bar{h} = \bar{u} + P\bar{v} \sim [\text{J/mol}]$$

## Page 6
Specific heat

[handwritten] → capacity of a substance to store energy

[handwritten] →
$$c_v = \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial T}\right)_v \sim \left[\frac{\text{J}}{\text{kg-K}}\right] \qquad \bar{c}_v = \left(\frac{\partial \bar{u}}{\partial T}\right)_{\bar{v}} \sim \left[\frac{\text{J}}{\text{mol-K}}\right]$$
$$c_p = \left(\frac{\partial h}{\partial T}\right)_P \sim \left[\frac{\text{J}}{\text{kg-K}}\right] \qquad \bar{c}_p = \left(\frac{\partial \bar{h}}{\partial T}\right)_P \sim \left[\frac{\text{J}}{\text{mol-K}}\right]$$
