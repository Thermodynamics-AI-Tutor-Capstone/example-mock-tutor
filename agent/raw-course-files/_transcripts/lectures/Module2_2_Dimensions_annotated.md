## Page 3
Derived dimensions and dimensional analysis

[handwritten] Left column:
- Pressure - P - Pa
- Volume - $\forall$ - m³ (script V / $\forall$ = total volume)
- Velocity - v - m/s
- density - $\rho$ - kg/m³

Right side:
$$P = \frac{F}{A} = \frac{Ma}{A}$$
$$\text{Pa} = \frac{\text{N}}{\text{m}^2} = \frac{\text{kg}\,\text{m/s}^2}{\text{m}^2} = \frac{\text{kg}}{\text{m}\cdot\text{s}^2}$$
