## Page 3

Example: Carnot efficiency

What is the greatest efficiency possible for a gas turbine where heat is added at 1200 K and the gas rejects heat to the atmosphere at 300 K?

[figure] Schematic of a heat engine: a box at the top labeled "[handwritten] $T_H = 1200\ \mathrm{K}$" with an arrow pointing down into an oval labeled "[handwritten] gas turbine"; from the oval an arrow points right labeled "[handwritten] $\dot{W}_{net}$", and an arrow points down to a box at the bottom labeled "[handwritten] $T_L = 300\ \mathrm{K}$".

[handwritten] $$\eta_{th,max} = 1 - \frac{T_L}{T_H} = 1 - \frac{300}{1200}$$

[handwritten] **ANSWER:** $\eta_{th,max} = 0.75$
