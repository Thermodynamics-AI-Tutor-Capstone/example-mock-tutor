## Page 2

Mass conservation

[handwritten] Finite time: $\Delta M_{sys} = M(t_2) - M(t_1) = M_{in} - M_{out} + \cancel{M_{gen}}$

[handwritten] $\rightarrow$ system: $\Delta M_{sys} = 0$

[handwritten] Instantaneous: $\underline{\underline{\dfrac{dM}{dt}\Big|_{cv}}} = \dot{m}_{in} - \dot{m}_{out} + \cancel{\dot{M}_{gen}}$ , $\dfrac{dM}{dt}\Big|_{sys} = 0$

[handwritten] $$\mathcal{V}_{av} = \frac{1}{A} \int_A \mathcal{V}\, dA$$

Mass flow example
