### Work input devices: compressors, fans, blowers

[handwritten]
Assume
- steady-flow ★
- $\Delta ke = 0$
- $\Delta pe = 0$
- $\dot{Q} = 0$

$$\dot{m}(\Delta h + \Delta ke + \Delta pe) = \dot{Q} - \dot{W}$$
(with $\Delta ke$, $\Delta pe$ and $\dot{Q}$ crossed out and marked 0)

Boxed: $$\dot{m}\Delta h = -\dot{W}$$

Arrow from the $-\dot{W}$ term: work done **on** fluid $< 0$; $d\mathcal{V} < 0$ (script V = total volume) [?], $\dot{W} < 0$, $\Delta h > 0$
