## Page 2
### Review of devices

[handwritten] Two columns.

**Passive ($\dot{W} = 0$)**
- → nozzles – $V\uparrow$ – $\Delta pe = \dot{Q} = \dot{W} = 0$
- → diffusers – $V\downarrow$ – $\Delta pe = \dot{Q} = \dot{W} = 0$
- → throttles – $P\downarrow$ – $\Delta ke = \Delta pe = \dot{Q} = \dot{W} = 0$
- → heat exchangers – $\dot{Q}$ – $\Delta ke = \Delta pe = \dot{W} = 0$

**Work Input/output**
- → compressor – work in – $\Delta ke = \Delta pe = \dot{Q} = 0$
- → turbine – work out – $\Delta ke = \Delta pe = \dot{Q} = 0$
