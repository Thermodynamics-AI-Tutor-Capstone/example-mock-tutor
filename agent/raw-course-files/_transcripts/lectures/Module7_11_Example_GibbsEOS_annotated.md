Example

A rigid tank with 0.3 kg of nitrogen (N2) begins at a pressure of 506625 Pa and a temperature of 400 K. Heat is removed until the temperature is equal to 300 K. Calculate the change in entropy of the nitrogen. Properties of N2: MW=28 kg/kmol, cp=1040 J/kg-K, cv=743 J/kg-K.

[handwritten] ✓
[handwritten] Given : 0.3 kg
[handwritten] P₁ = 506625 Pa
[handwritten] T₁ = 400 K, T₂ = 300 K

[handwritten] Find : ΔS ~ [J/k]

[handwritten] Assume : ideal gas
[handwritten] C_v, C_p = const
[handwritten] quasi-eq :
[handwritten] ΔT ≠ 0 , M = const
[handwritten] Δv = 0

[handwritten] ΔS = C_v ln(T₂/T₁) + R ln(V₂/V₁) ~ [J/kg-K]

[handwritten] ⇒ ΔS = C_p ln(T₂/T₁) − R ln(P₂/P₁)

[handwritten] ΔS = C_v ln(T₂/T₁) = 743 ln(300/400) = −213.7 J/kg-K

[handwritten] ΔS = M Δs

[handwritten] **[boxed]** **ANSWER:** ΔS = −64.12 kJ

[handwritten] P₂ = RT₂/V₂     v₂ = v₁
[handwritten] v₁ = RT₁/P₁
[handwritten] R = R̄/MW
