## Page 2
Energy, enthalpy, specific heat

[handwritten] Left column:
$$U \sim [\text{J}], \quad u \sim [\text{J/kg}], \quad \bar{u} \sim [\text{J/mol}]$$
$$H = U + PV \sim [\text{J}]$$ (script V = total volume)
$$h = u + Pv \sim [\text{J/kg}]$$
$$\bar{h} = \bar{u} + P\bar{v} \sim [\text{J/mol}]$$

[handwritten] Right column (separated by a vertical line):
$$c_v = \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial T}\right)_v \sim [\text{J/kg-K}]$$
$$c_p = \left(\frac{\partial h}{\partial T}\right)_P$$

## Page 3
Calorific equations of state

[handwritten] (top right) S.C.S. → $u = u(T, v)$; $u = u(T)$

[handwritten] If ideal gas (underlined) → $u = u(T)$, $h = h(T)$

[handwritten]
$$\Rightarrow c_v = \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial T}\right)_v = \frac{du}{dT} \longrightarrow du = c_v dT$$
(small arrow under the subscript $v$)
$$du = c_v(T)\,dT$$
$$\Delta u = \int_{T_1}^{T_2} c_v(T)\,dT \xrightarrow{c_v = \text{const}} \Delta u = c_v \Delta T$$
(the "$c_v$ = const" condition is labelled with an arrow: "calorically perfect gas"; $\Delta u = c_v\Delta T$ double-underlined)

[handwritten]
$$\Rightarrow c_p = \left(\frac{\partial h}{\partial T}\right)_P = \frac{dh}{dT} \longrightarrow dh = c_p dT$$
$$dh = c_p(T)\,dT$$
$$\Delta h = \int_{T_1}^{T_2} c_p(T)\,dT \xrightarrow{c_p = \text{const.}} \Delta h = c_p \Delta T$$
($\Delta h = c_p \Delta T$ double-underlined)
