Generalized compressibility

[handwritten] $Pv = RT$

[handwritten] $$Z = \frac{Pv}{RT}$$

[figure] Handwritten $Z$–$P$ plot. Vertical axis labelled [handwritten] $Z$ (with an arrow pointing up), horizontal axis labelled [handwritten] $P$ (with an arrow pointing right). Tick marks on the $P$ axis labelled [handwritten] 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. A horizontal dashed line extends to the right along $Z = 1$, labelled [handwritten] $Pv=RT$. Three solid curves start at the same point on the $Z$ axis ($Z = 1$) and decrease with increasing $P$: the topmost curve is labelled [handwritten] $N_2$, the middle curve is labelled [handwritten] $H_2O$, and the lowest curve (decreasing most steeply) is labelled [handwritten] $CO_2$.

## Page 3

Example: Generalized compressibility

[handwritten] He @ 500 K 1 atm = 101325 Pa
[handwritten] $\rightarrow v = 10.263\ \mathrm{m^3/kg}$
[handwritten] $\rightarrow R = \dfrac{\bar{R}}{M} = 2078.62\ \dfrac{J}{kg\cdot K}$

[handwritten] $$Z = \frac{Pv}{RT} = \frac{(101325)(10.263)}{(2078.62)(500)}$$
[handwritten] $$= 1$$

[handwritten] H$_2$O  500 k  2 MPa
[handwritten] $\underline{Z = 0.9}$
[handwritten] [figure] A short downward-pointing arrow drawn beneath the underlined $Z = 0.9$.

[handwritten] $P_c$ $T_c$ $\rightarrow$ liquids and gases are no longer distinguishable
