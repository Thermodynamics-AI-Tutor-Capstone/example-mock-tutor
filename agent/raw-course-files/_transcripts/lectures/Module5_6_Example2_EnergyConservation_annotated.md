## Slide 4

Energy Conservation

$$\Delta E = {}_{1}Q_{2} - {}_{1}\overline{W}_{2} \;\longrightarrow\; \Delta KE = \Delta PE = 0$$

$$\longrightarrow \Delta \overline{U} = 0 \;\longrightarrow\; {}_{1}\overline{W}_{2} = 0$$

$$\Delta \overline{U} = {}_{1}Q_{2} \;\sim\; [\text{J}]$$

$${}_{1}Q_{2} = \Delta \overline{U} = M\,\Delta \underline{u} \qquad \xleftarrow{\;[\text{J/kg}]\;}$$

$$\mathcal{V} = M v \;\Rightarrow\; M = \frac{\mathcal{V}}{v_{1}}$$

[handwritten] State 1 : $v_{1},\, u_{1} \rightarrow M$

[handwritten] State 2 : $u_{2}$
