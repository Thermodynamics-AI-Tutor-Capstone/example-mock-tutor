## Page 2
Finite time energy conservation

[handwritten]
$\Delta E_{sys} = E_{in} - E_{out} + E_{gen}$ ($E_{in}$, $E_{out}$ underlined; $E_{gen}$ struck through with "→ 0")  →  $E_{sys} = KE + PE + U$

$dE_{sys} = (\delta Q_{in} + \delta W_{in}) - (\delta Q_{out} + \delta W_{out})$ (the "d" and "δ" underlined)
$= (\delta Q_{in} - \delta Q_{out}) + (\delta W_{in} - \delta W_{out})$
$= (\delta Q_{in} - \delta Q_{out}) - (\delta W_{out} - \delta W_{in})$
$dE_{sys} = \delta Q_{in,net} - \delta W_{out,net}$

**ANSWER:** $\Delta E_{sys} = \Delta KE + \Delta PE + \Delta U = {}_1Q_2 - {}_1W_2$ (boxed)

(side note) $W_{out} > 0$  $dV > 0$ (script V = total volume)
$W_{in} < 0$  $dV < 0$
