Quasi-equilibrium

[handwritten] Quasi-static process is one that happens sufficiently slowly
[handwritten] that the time for the system to achieve equilibrium is
[handwritten] much shorter that the timescale of the process.

[handwritten] $$t_{ef} << t_{process}$$
