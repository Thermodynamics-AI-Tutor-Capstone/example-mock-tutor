## Page 2

Heat pump example

A heat pump is used to heat a house. The outside air is T=-10 °C and the inside is a constant 20.3 °C. The heat loss through the roof of the house is a steady 30 kW. Determine the minimum power required to run a heat pump to keep the house at this temperature.

[figure] A house at the top with a wavy arrow labelled $\dot{Q}_{loss} = 30\ \text{kW}$; a heat pump circle below it with an arrow upward into the house labelled $\dot{Q}_H$; a horizontal arrow into the heat pump from the right labelled $\dot{P}$; an arrow upward into the heat pump from a trapezoidal ground shape below labelled $\dot{Q}_L$. Handwritten labels: $T_H$ to the left of the house, $T_L$ to the left of the ground shape.

[handwritten] $T_H = 20.3\,^\circ\text{C} = 293.45\ \text{K}$

[handwritten] $T_L = -10\,^\circ\text{C} = 263.15\ \text{K}$

[handwritten] $T_H = \text{const} \Rightarrow \dot{Q}_H = \dot{Q}_{loss} = 30\ \text{kW}$

## Page 4

Heat pump example

[handwritten] $$\beta = \frac{\dot{Q}_H}{\dot{W}}$$

[handwritten] $$\beta_{max} = \beta_{Carnot} = \frac{T_H}{T_H - T_L} = \frac{293.45}{293.45 - 268.15} = 9.68$$

[handwritten] $$\dot{W} = \frac{\dot{Q}_H}{\beta_{max}} = \frac{30\ \text{kW}}{9.68} \implies \boxed{\dot{W}_{min} = 3.1\ \text{kW}}$$

**ANSWER:** $\dot{W}_{min} = 3.1\ \text{kW}$
