## Page 5
### Thermal efficiency

[handwritten]
$$\eta_{th} = \frac{\text{net work}}{\text{heat in}} \quad \text{★ cycle}$$
("heat in" double-underlined; "cycle" underlined)

$$\eta_{th} = \frac{W_{net}}{Q_H} = \frac{Q_H - Q_L}{Q_H} = 1 - \frac{Q_L}{Q_H}$$

Boxed:
$$\eta_{th} = 1 - \frac{Q_L}{Q_H}$$
