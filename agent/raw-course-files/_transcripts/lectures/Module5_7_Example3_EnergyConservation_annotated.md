## Slide 10

Process 3-1

[handwritten] $d\mathcal{V} < 0 \rightarrow {}_3\overline{W}_1 < 0$

[handwritten] $${}_3\overline{W}_1 = \int_{\mathcal{V}_3}^{\mathcal{V}_1} P\,d\mathcal{V} \rightarrow P\mathcal{V} = MRT$$

[handwritten] $$P = \frac{MRT}{\mathcal{V}}$$

[handwritten] $$= \int_{\mathcal{V}_3}^{\mathcal{V}_1} \frac{MRT}{\mathcal{V}}\,d\mathcal{V}$$

[handwritten] $$= MRT \int_{\mathcal{V}_3}^{\mathcal{V}_1} \frac{1}{\mathcal{V}}\,d\mathcal{V} = MRT \ln\left(\frac{\mathcal{V}_1}{\mathcal{V}_3}\right)$$

[handwritten] $$= (1)(287)(300)\ln\left(\frac{0.431}{1.148}\right) \Rightarrow {}_3\overline{W}_1 = -8\,4344.5\ \text{J}$$
