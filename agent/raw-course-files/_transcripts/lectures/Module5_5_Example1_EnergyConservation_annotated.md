## Page 2
A closed piston-cylinder system filled with air rejects 25 kJ of energy in a heat-interaction while experiencing a volume change of 0.1 m³ (from 0.15 to 0.05 m³). Assuming a reversible, constant-pressure process at 350 kPa, determine the change in energy of the air inside.

[handwritten]
Given: $M$ = const.
${}_1Q_2 = -25000$ J
$V_1 = 0.15$ m³ (script V = total volume)
$V_2 = 0.05$ m³
$P_2 = P_1 = 350000$ Pa
Find: $\Delta E = \Delta U$

Assume: ideal gas
: quasi-equilibrium
: $\Delta KE = \Delta PE = 0$

⇒ $\Delta U = {}_1Q_2 - {}_1W_2$ (each term underlined)

## Page 4
Energy Conservation

[handwritten]
$\Delta U = {}_1Q_2 - {}_1W_2$  →  ${}_1W_2 = \displaystyle\int_{V_1}^{V_2} P\,dV = P\int_{V_1}^{V_2} dV = P(V_2 - V_1)$
$= 350000\,(0.05 - 0.15)$
$= -35000$

$\Delta U = -25000 - (-35000)$
**ANSWER:** $= 10000$ J
