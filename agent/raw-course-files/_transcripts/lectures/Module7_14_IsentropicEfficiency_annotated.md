Isentropic efficiency – turbine

[handwritten]
Purpose: produce work

$$\eta_{isen,t} = \frac{\dot{W}_{real}}{\dot{W}_{ideal}} < 1, \quad \dot{W}_{real} < \dot{W}_{ideal}$$

- arrow to $\dot{W}_{real}$: "work done by real turbine, $\Phi > 0$" [?] (symbol looks like a capital Phi; likely the instructor's symbol for entropy generation)
- arrow to $\dot{W}_{ideal}$: "work done by a reversible, adiabatic turbine" — "reversible, adiabatic" underbraced → $\Delta s = 0$

$$\Rightarrow \eta_{isen,t} \approx 70\text{–}90\%$$

## Page 7
Isentropic efficiency – compressor

[handwritten]
$$\eta_{isen,comp} = \frac{\dot{W}_{ideal}}{\dot{W}_{real}} < 1, \quad \dot{W}_{ideal} < \dot{W}_{real}, \quad \eta_{isen,comp} = 75\text{–}85\%$$
(the efficiency definition is double-underlined)

$$\Rightarrow \dot{m}\,\Delta h = -\dot{W} \;\longrightarrow\; \begin{cases} \dot{W}_{real} = -\dot{m}(h_2 - h_1) & \longrightarrow \text{state 2, } s_2 > s_1,\ P_2 \\ \dot{W}_{ideal} = -\dot{m}(h_{2,s} - h_1) & \end{cases}$$

($s_2$ in "$s_2 > s_1$" and $h_{2,s}$ are double-underlined)

where $h_{2,s}$ is the enthalpy 2s where $s_{2,s} = s_1$ (ideal), $P_2$

→ turb: $h_{2,s} < h_2$ – less work extracted in real turb.
→ comp: $h_{2,s} < h_2$ – less work input in ideal turbine

[transcriber note: the "comp" line says "ideal turbine"; from context the instructor means the ideal compressor.]
