## Page 2

Non-ideal gases

[handwritten] Ideal gases $\longrightarrow$ $\bar{v}_{\text{mol}} \ll \ll \bar{v}_{\text{blw mol}}$
[handwritten] $\longrightarrow$ no intermolecular forces

[handwritten] Non-ideals $\longrightarrow$ density is high, but $T$ isn't really
[handwritten] $\longrightarrow$ intermolecular forces are important

## Page 3

Van der Waals equation of state

[handwritten] $P\mathcal{V} = NR_u T$

[handwritten] $P\bar{v} = R_u T$ → $\bar{v} \sim \left[\frac{\mathrm{m}^3}{\mathrm{kmol}}\right]$

[handwritten] $\Rightarrow \boxed{\left(P + \frac{a}{\bar{v}^2}\right)\left(\bar{v} - b\right) = R_u T}$

[handwritten] a – accounts for intermolecular forces
[handwritten] b – accounts for volume of mol.

## Page 4

Example — Van der Waals EOS

[handwritten] $\mathrm{CH_4} \rightarrow M = 16.04\ ^{kg}\!/\!_{kmol} \rightarrow \bar{R} = \dfrac{\bar{R}_u}{M} = 518.4\ \dfrac{J}{kg\,K}$

[handwritten] $T = 300\ K$

[handwritten] $\rho = 175\ ^{kg}\!/\!_{m^3}$

[handwritten] $\rightarrow$ ideal gas: $P = \rho R T = (175)(518.4)(300)$ $\quad$ **ANSWER:** $P_{ideal\ gas} = \boxed{27.2\ \text{MPa}}$

[handwritten] $\rightarrow$ V.d.W.: $v = \dfrac{1}{\rho} = 0.0057\ m^3\!/\!_{kg}$ , $\bar{v} = v\,M = 0.0917\ m^3\!/\!_{kmol}$

[handwritten] $$\left(P + \dfrac{a}{\bar{v}^2}\right)(\bar{v} - b) = \bar{R}_u T$$

[handwritten] $$P = \dfrac{\bar{R}_u T}{\bar{v} - b} - \dfrac{a}{\bar{v}^2} = \dfrac{(8314.5)(300)}{(0.0917 - 0.0427)} - \dfrac{2.285\times 10^5}{(0.0917)^2} = \text{**ANSWER:** } \boxed{23.7\ \text{MPa}}$$
