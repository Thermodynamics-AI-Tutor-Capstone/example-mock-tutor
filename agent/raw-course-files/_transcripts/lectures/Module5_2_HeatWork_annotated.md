## Page 2
Heat

[handwritten]
→ NOT A PROPERTY ("PROPERTY" underlined)
→ exchange of energy to/from system from/to the surrounding, without transfer of mass, due to a temperature difference ("temperature difference" underlined)
- occurs at boundaries

⇒ $Q$ = heat [J]
$\dot{Q}$ = heat transfer rate [J/s] ~ [W]
$\dot{Q}''$ = heat flux ~ [W/m²]

⇒ Adiabatic: $Q = 0$

## Page 3
Work

[handwritten]
⇒ NOT A PROPERTY
⇒ ${}_1W_2 = \oint \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{s}$ [transcriber note: written with a closed-contour-style integral sign as shown; path from state 1 to 2]
${}_1W_2 \sim$ [J]

$\delta W = \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{s}$ (the δ double underlined) — inexact differential ↳ path dependent
$dT$ – exact differential

⇒ $\mathcal{P} = \dfrac{\text{work}}{\text{time}} = \dot{W} = \vec{F} \cdot \dfrac{d\vec{s}}{dt} = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{\mathcal{V}} \sim$ [J/s] ~ [W]   (script P = power; script V = velocity)

[figure] Two points, "1" (at temperature $T_1$) and "2" (at $T_2$), joined by two different curved paths (upper and lower). On the upper path a small displacement $d\vec{s}$ and a force vector $\vec{F}$ are drawn. Illustrates that work depends on path while the change in T depends only on end states.

Types of work

[handwritten]
1. Compression/expansion work

$\delta W = F\,ds$ ⇒ $P = \dfrac{F}{A}$, $A\,ds = dV$ (script V = total volume)
$\delta W = P\,dV$
**ANSWER:** ${}_1W_2 = \displaystyle\int_{V_1}^{V_2} P\,dV$ (boxed)

Compression: $dV < 0$, ${}_1W_2 = \displaystyle\int_{V_1}^{V_2} P\,dV < 0$  work done on system: – ("on" double underlined)
Expansion: $dV > 0$, ${}_1W_2 = \displaystyle\int_{V_1}^{V_2} P\,dV > 0$  work done by system + ("by" underlined)
(bracket around both lines with a star drawn beside it — emphasis on the sign convention)
