## Slide 8

[handwritten]
**Given:** $M = 1\,\text{kg}$; P, T, v for all states
**Find:** ${}_1W_2 \ldots$, ${}_1Q_2 \ldots$, $W_{net}$, $Q_{net}$
**Assume:** S.C.S.
- quasi-eq. processes
- ${}_1Q_2 = {}_3Q_4 = 0$ (adiabatic)
- $\Delta KE = \Delta PE = 0$ ⇒ $\Delta U = Q - W$

**Strategy:**
$${}_1W_2 = \int_{\mathcal{V}_1}^{\mathcal{V}_2} P\,d\mathcal{V} = M\int_{v_1}^{v_2} P\,dv \ ★$$
$$\Delta U = M\Delta u = Q - W \rightarrow Q = M\Delta u + W$$ ($\Delta u$ double-underlined)
