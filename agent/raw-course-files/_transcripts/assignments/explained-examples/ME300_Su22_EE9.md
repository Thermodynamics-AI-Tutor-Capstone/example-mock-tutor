## Page 1

**EXPLAINED EXAMPLE 9 – COMBINED CYCLE**

① A small engine runs with a thermal efficiency of 0.3. Because the efficiency is so low, the rejected heat is used to run a refrigeration cycle with a coefficient of performance of 4. Draw the heat engine diagram for this configuration, labeling the heat and work flows for the two devices. Then calculate the ratio of the heat input to the engine to the heat input to the refrigerator.

[handwritten] *Given* : $\eta_{th} = 0.3$
[handwritten] $\beta = 4$

[handwritten] *Find* : $\dfrac{\dot{Q}_{in,eng}}{\dot{Q}_{in,frig}}$

[handwritten] *Assume* : Steady operation
[handwritten] ① : no energy losses to the outside

[handwritten] → *Diagram* :

[figure] Two devices side by side. Left device: a box labeled "Eng" (engine). Above it a box labeled "T$_{H,1}$" with a circled ② above it. An arrow labeled $\dot{Q}_{in,eng}$ points down from T$_{H,1}$ into Eng. An arrow labeled $\dot{W}_{net}$ points right from Eng toward the right device. An arrow labeled $\dot{Q}_{out,eng}$ points down out of Eng into a box labeled "T$_{L,1}$". Right device: a box labeled "frig" (refrigerator). An arrow labeled $\dot{Q}_{in,frig}$ points up into frig from a box labeled "T$_{L,2}$" below. An arrow labeled $\dot{Q}_{out,frig}$ points up out of frig into a box labeled "T$_{H,2}$" above, with a circled ① above that box. The $\dot{W}_{net}$ arrow from Eng enters frig.

② [handwritten] *efficiency* : $\eta_{th} = \dfrac{\dot{W}_{net}}{\dot{Q}_{in,eng}}$

[handwritten] *COP* : $\beta = \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{in,frig}}{\dot{W}_{net}}$  [handwritten] b/c point of a frig is to remove heat from a cold space

## Page 2

[handwritten] $\rightarrow$ ratio : $\dot{W}_{net} = \eta_{th}\,\dot{Q}_{in,eng}$

[handwritten] ① : $\beta = \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{in,fng}}{\eta_{th}\,\dot{Q}_{in,eng}} \;\Rightarrow\; \eta_{th}\,\beta = \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{in,fng}}{\dot{Q}_{in,eng}}$

[handwritten] $\Rightarrow \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{in,eng}}{\dot{Q}_{in,fng}} = \dfrac{1}{\eta_{th}\,\beta} = \dfrac{1}{(0.3)(4)} \;\Rightarrow\;$ **ANSWER:** $\boxed{0.83}$
