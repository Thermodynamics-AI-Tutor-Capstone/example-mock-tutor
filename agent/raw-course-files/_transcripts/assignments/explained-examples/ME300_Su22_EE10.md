## Page 1
**EXPLAINED EXAMPLE 10 – TURBOJET ANALYSIS**

[handwritten] (green circled "1" beside the problem statement; circled "1"/"2" step markers in green and purple appear throughout the solution)

A turbojet aircraft engine at altitude (T=230 K and P=18700 Pa) is flying at 250 m/s with a mass flow of 680 kg/s through the engine. The inlet diffuser slows the flow down to a velocity of 125 m/s. The pressure ratio of the compressor is 25 and the isentropic efficiency of the compressor is 0.9. Heat is added at a rate of 200 MJ/sec. What is the temperature of the air coming out the back of the turbine? Assume $c_p = 1001\text{ J/kg-K}$ and $\gamma = 1.4$.

[handwritten]
Given: (2)
- $T_1 = 230\text{ K}$
- $P_1 = 18700\text{ Pa}$
- $\mathcal{V}_1 = 250\text{ m/s}$ (script V = velocity here)
- $\dot{m} = 680\text{ kg/s}$
- $\mathcal{V}_2 = 125\text{ m/s}$
- $P_3/P_2 = 25$
- $\eta_c = 0.9$
- $\dot{Q}_{in} = 200\text{ MJ/s}$

Find: $T_5$

Assume: (1)
- ideal gas
- steady flow
- nozzle: $\Delta pe = \dot{Q} = \dot{W} = 0$
- comp: $\Delta ke = \Delta pe = \dot{Q} = 0$
- comb: $\Delta ke = \Delta pe = \dot{W} = 0$
- turb: $\Delta ke = \Delta pe = \dot{Q} = 0$
- ideal cycle → reversible

⇒ diagram (2)

[figure] Turbojet schematic, flow $\dot{m}$ entering from the left: state (1) at inlet of the diffuser (diverging section labelled "diffuser"); state (2) between diffuser and compressor; converging section labelled "compressor"; state (3) at compressor exit; a straight duct labelled "combustor"; state (4) at combustor exit / turbine inlet; diverging section labelled "turbine"; state (5) at turbine exit; final section labelled "nozzle"; state (6) at nozzle exit.

State 1 (1): $T_1 = 230\text{ K}$, $P_1 = 18700\text{ Pa}$, $\mathcal{V}_1 = 250\text{ m/s}$

## Page 2
[handwritten]
State 2 (2): diffuser: $\dot{m}(\Delta h + \Delta ke + \Delta pe) = \dot{Q} - \dot{W}$ (with $\Delta pe$, $\dot{Q}$, $\dot{W}$ struck → 0)
$$\Rightarrow \Delta h + \Delta ke = 0$$
$$c_p(T_2 - T_1) + \frac{1}{2}(\mathcal{V}_2^2 - \mathcal{V}_1^2) = 0$$
$$T_2 = T_1 - \frac{1}{2c_p}(\mathcal{V}_2^2 - \mathcal{V}_1^2) = 0$$
$$= 250 - \frac{1}{2002}(250^2 - 125^2) \rightarrow$$
**ANSWER:** $T_2 = 226.6\text{ K}$

[transcriber note: the page substitutes 250 for $T_1$ (the given $T_1$ is 230 K), and writes the squared velocities as $(250^2 - 125^2)$, i.e. $\mathcal{V}_1^2 - \mathcal{V}_2^2$, with a minus sign in front. With $T_1 = 230$ the same expression gives about 206.6 K. The "= 0" at the end of the $T_2$ line is as written. Transcribed as written; downstream numbers use 226.6 K.]

(1) State 3s: Isentropic compression
$$T_{3,s} = T_2\left(\frac{P_3}{P_2}\right)^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}} = 226.6(25)^{\frac{0.4}{1.4}} \rightarrow$$
**ANSWER:** $T_{3,s} = 568.4\text{ K}$

(2) State 3:
$$\eta_{comp} = \frac{\dot{W}_{ideal}}{\dot{W}_{real}} = \frac{-\dot{m}c_p(T_{3,s} - T_2)}{-\dot{m}c_p(T_3 - T_2)}$$
$$T_3 = T_2 + \frac{(T_{3,s} - T_2)}{\eta_{comp}} = 226.6 + \frac{(568.4 - 226.6)}{0.9}$$
**ANSWER:** $T_3 = 606.38\text{ K}$

(1) State 4: combustor → $\dot{m}(\Delta h + \Delta ke + \Delta pe) = \dot{Q} - \dot{W}$ (with $\Delta ke$, $\Delta pe$, $\dot{W}$ struck → 0)
$$\dot{m}c_p(T_4 - T_3) = \dot{Q}$$
$$T_4 = T_3 + \frac{\dot{Q}}{\dot{m}c_p} = 606.38 + \frac{200 \times 10^6}{(680)(1001)}$$
**ANSWER:** $T_4 = 900.2\text{ K}$

## Page 3
[handwritten]
(2) State 5: Turbine drives compressor $\dot{W}_{turb} = -\dot{W}_{comp}$
$$\Rightarrow -\dot{m}c_p(T_5 - T_4) = -\left[-\dot{m}c_p(T_3 - T_2)\right]$$
($\dot{m}$ and $c_p$ struck out as cancelling)
$$T_5 = T_4 - (T_3 - T_2)$$
$$= 900.2 - (606.38 - 226.6)$$
**ANSWER:** $T_5 = 520.4\text{ K}$
