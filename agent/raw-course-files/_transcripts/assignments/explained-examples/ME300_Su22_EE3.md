**EXPLAINED EXAMPLE 3 – ENERGY CONSERVATION WITH PHASE CHANGE**

① Initially, 0.454 kg of water at 420 K with a quality of 0.6 is contained in a piston-cylinder device. The piston is then slowly moved until the water is entirely converted into liquid. Heat transfer maintains the temperature at 420 K. Calculate:

② a) The change in volume in meters cubed

b) The work in kJ

c) The heat in kJ

b) $\underset{\textcircled{1}}{{}_1W_2 = \int_{\mathcal{V}_1}^{\mathcal{V}_2} P\,d\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow$ inside vapor dome, isothermal = isobaric
$\rightarrow$ Table D1
$= P\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{V}_1}^{\mathcal{V}_2} d\mathcal{V} = P_{sat}(\mathcal{V}_2 - \mathcal{V}_1) = (0.437309\times10^{4})(-0.1155)$

$\boxed{{}_1W_2 = -50552.9\ \text{J}}$

c) $\underset{\textcircled{2}}{\text{First law:}\quad M\Delta u = {}_1Q_2 - {}_1W_2}$
${}_1Q_2 = M\Delta u + {}_1W_2$

State 1: $u_1 = x_1 u_g + (1-x_1)u_f$
$= (0.6)(2556.2) + (0.4)(618.13) = 1780.97\ \dfrac{\text{kJ}}{\text{kg}}$

State 2: $u_2 = u_f = 618.13\ \dfrac{\text{kJ}}{\text{kg}}$

$\Rightarrow M(u_2 - u_1) = (0.457)(618.13 - 1780.97) = -527.93\ \text{kJ}$

$\Rightarrow {}_1Q_2 = M\Delta u + {}_1W_2$
$= -527.93 - 50.5529$

$\boxed{{}_1Q_2 = -578.5\ \text{kJ}}$
