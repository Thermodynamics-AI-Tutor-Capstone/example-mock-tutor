**EXPLAINED EXAMPLE 6 – CARNOT CYCLE**

① A factory has an engine that runs a reversible Carnot cycle, detailed below. The engine runs between two temperature reservoirs at 1200 K and 300 K.

- Process 1-2: Isentropic compression
- Process 2-3: Isothermal heat addition
- Process 3-4: Isentropic expansion
- Process 4-1: Isothermal heat rejection

② a) Draw a heat engine diagram of this cycle, identifying the reservoirs and heat and work flows.
b) What is the thermal efficiency of this cycle?
c) An 800 kW heater is used to provide the heat for process 2-3. What is the net work produced by the engine?
d) What is the rate of change of entropy of the fluid during each of the four processes (dS/dt in kJ/K-s)?

[handwritten] Given : $T_H = 1200\ \text{K}$
[handwritten] $T_L = 300\ \text{K}$
[handwritten] $\dot{Q}_{in} = 800\ \text{kW}$

[handwritten] Find : $\eta_{th}$
[handwritten] : $\dot{W}_{net}$
[handwritten] : $\dot{S}$

[handwritten] Assume : ideal (reversible) cycle
[handwritten] : quasi-steady

[handwritten] a)
[figure] Heat engine diagram: a box labeled "$T_H$" at the top; a downward arrow from $T_H$ labeled "$\dot{Q}_{in}$" pointing into a circle labeled "cycle"; a rightward arrow out of the cycle labeled "$\dot{W}_{net}$"; a downward arrow out of the cycle labeled "$\dot{Q}_{out}$" pointing into a box labeled "$T_L$" at the bottom.

[handwritten] b) $\eta_{th} = \eta_{Carnot} = 1 - \dfrac{T_L}{T_H}$
[handwritten] $= 1 - \dfrac{300}{1200}$

[handwritten] **ANSWER:** $\boxed{\eta_{th} = 0.75}$

[handwritten] c) $\eta_{th} = \dfrac{\dot{W}_{net}}{\dot{Q}_{in}} \rightarrow \dot{W}_{net} = \eta_{th}\,\dot{Q}_{in} = (0.75)(800)$

[handwritten] **ANSWER:** $\boxed{\dot{W}_{net} = 600\ \text{kW}}$

d) Process 1-2 : isentropic compression

(②) $\rightarrow \boxed{\dot{S} = 0\ \frac{\text{W}}{\text{K}}}$

Process 2-3 : Isothermal heat in

$\rightarrow \dot{S} = \int \left.\left(\dfrac{\delta \dot{Q}}{T}\right)\right|_{rev}$

$= \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{in}}{T_H} = \dfrac{800}{1200}\ \dfrac{\text{kW}}{\text{K}}$

$\boxed{\dot{S} = 0.67\ \dfrac{\text{kW}}{\text{K}}}$

(①) Process 3-4 : isentropic expansion

$\rightarrow \boxed{\dot{S} = 0\ \dfrac{\text{W}}{\text{K}}}$

Process 4-1 : isothermal heat out

$\rightarrow \dot{S} = \int \left.\left(\dfrac{\delta \dot{Q}}{T}\right)\right|_{rev}$

$= \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{out}}{T_L} \qquad \rightarrow \quad \dot{Q}_{out} = \dot{Q}_{in} - \dot{W}_{net}$

$\phantom{= \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{out}}{T_L} \qquad \rightarrow \quad} = 800 - 600$

$\phantom{= \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{out}}{T_L} \qquad \rightarrow \quad} = 200\ \text{kW}$
$\phantom{= \dfrac{\dot{Q}_{out}}{T_L} \qquad \rightarrow \quad \text{heat out} < 0}$

$= -\dfrac{200}{300}$

$\boxed{\dot{S} = -0.67\ \dfrac{\text{kW}}{\text{K}}}$
