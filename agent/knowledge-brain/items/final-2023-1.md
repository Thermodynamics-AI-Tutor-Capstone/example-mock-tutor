# Final exam (Summer 2023), problem 1: COP of reversible refrigerator from cold to hot day

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

## Problem

A refrigerator is set to keep a constant temperature in the cold-space. Assuming the refrigeration cycle is reversible, how would the coefficient of performance of that refrigerator change from a cold day to a hot day? Use an equation as part of your explanation.

## What it tests

- Students can apply the reversible refrigerator COP formula and predict the effect of changing the warm-side temperature.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- The refrigeration cycle is reversible.
- The cold-space temperature is constant.
- A hot day increases T_H, the temperature of the room around the refrigerator.

## Where students go wrong

- Treating COP as an efficiency and assuming it cannot exceed 1.
- Thinking COP increases on a hot day.
- Using T_H as the cold-space temperature.
- Not using the reversible/Carnot COP expression.

## How it is graded

Solutions award +3 for the Carnot COP equation and +3 for explaining that increasing T_H lowers COP for fixed T_L.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- COP: decreases as T_H increases
- $COP_{Carnot}$: \frac{T_L}{T_H - T_L}
