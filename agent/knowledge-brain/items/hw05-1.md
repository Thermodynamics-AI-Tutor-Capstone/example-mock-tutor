## Problem

What does the incompressible assumption mean? Explain it in your own words. (10 points)

## What it tests

- Students can explain the incompressible substance assumption in their own words.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Saying $v$ and $u$ are independent of temperature rather than pressure
- Applying the incompressible approximation to gases or vapors
- Treating incompressible as only constant density while still allowing internal energy to depend strongly on pressure

## How it is graded

+10 for clearly stating that $v$ and $u$ are much more sensitive to temperature than pressure and for giving the saturated-liquid approximation.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Incompressible assumption: $v$ and $u$ are much more sensitive to changes in $T$ than in $P$; $v(T,P) \approx v(T) \approx v_f(T_{sat}=T)$ and $u(T,P) \approx u(T) \approx u_f(T_{sat}=T)$; applies to liquids
