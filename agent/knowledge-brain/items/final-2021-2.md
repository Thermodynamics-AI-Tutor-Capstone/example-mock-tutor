# Final exam (Summer 2021), problem 2: Otto versus Diesel thermal efficiency at equal compression ratio

## Problem

For a given compression ratio, initial pressure and temperature, and heat addition, which cycle has a higher thermal efficiency? OTTO CYCLE or DIESEL CYCLE. Explain your response. (6 points)

## What it tests

- Students can compare Otto and Diesel cycles under equal compression ratio and heat addition by reasoning about the heat-addition process and net work.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Same compression ratio, initial pressure and temperature, and same heat addition for both cycles.

## Where students go wrong

- Choosing Diesel because it is often associated with higher compression ratios, even though compression ratio is fixed here.
- Overlooking that Otto heat addition is isochoric, so expansion begins from a higher pressure and produces more work for the same $Q_{in}$.
- Not using $\eta = W_{net}/Q_{in}$ to complete the comparison.

## How it is graded

+2 for correct choice OTTO; +2 for explaining isochoric heat addition; +2 for linking higher expansion pressure/work to efficiency.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Correct cycle: OTTO CYCLE
