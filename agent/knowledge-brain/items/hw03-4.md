## Problem

4. (10 points) Explain the assumptions that must be met in order for a gas to be considered an ideal gas. What equation of state results from these assumptions?

## What it tests

- State the molecular assumptions of the ideal-gas model
- Write the ideal-gas equation of state

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Molecules are very far apart
- All collisions are elastic

## Where students go wrong

- Stating only macroscopic conditions such as low pressure or high temperature without the molecular assumptions
- Omitting that collisions are elastic
- Writing the mass form $PV=MRT$ when asked for the molar equation of state
- Using particular gas constant rather than universal gas constant in the mole form

## How it is graded

The solution awards +4 for the first molecular assumption, +4 for elastic collisions, and +2 for the equation of state.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Ideal gas assumptions: Molecules are very far apart; all collisions are elastic
- Equation of state: $PV=N\bar{R}T$
