## Problem

6. (20 points) An ideal gas undergoes a four-step cycle with the following steps. Calculate the change in mass-specific internal energy and mass-specific enthalpy for each of the steps and then the total by summing up all the changes. Explain the answer you get for the total change in internal energy and enthalpy. Assume $c_v=700$ J/kg-K and $c_p=900$ J/kg-K.
- State 1 − State 2: Isothermal compression starting at $T_1=300$ K
- State 2 − State 3: Isochoric heat addition to $T_3=800$ K
- State 3 − State 4: Isothermal expansion
- State 4 − State 1: Isochoric heat rejection

## What it tests

- Use $\Delta u=c_v\Delta T$ and $\Delta h=c_p\Delta T$ for ideal gas
- Evaluate isothermal and isochoric process steps
- Sum property changes around a cycle
- Explain zero net property change for a cycle

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Ideal gas
- Constant specific heats $c_v$ and $c_p$
- $T_2=T_1=300$ K from isothermal compression
- $T_4=T_3=800$ K from isothermal expansion

## Where students go wrong

- Using $c_p$ for $\Delta u$ or $c_v$ for $\Delta h$
- Not recognizing that isothermal ideal-gas steps have zero $\Delta u$ and zero $\Delta h$
- Using wrong temperatures, e.g. not identifying $T_2=300$ K and $T_4=800$ K
- Getting the sign wrong for 4-1 heat rejection; $\Delta u$ and $\Delta h$ are negative because temperature drops from 800 K to 300 K
- Treating the total $\Delta u$ or $\Delta h$ around the cycle as nonzero or path-dependent
- Unit errors between J/kg and kJ/kg when multiplying 700 or 900 J/kg-K by 500 K

## How it is graded

The solution awards +1 for ideal-gas assumption, +1 for constant specific heats, +3+1 for each isothermal $\Delta u/\Delta h$ relation and zero value, +1 for each nonzero 2-3 value, +1 for each nonzero 4-1 value, +1 each for totals, and +2 for explaining that properties return to the original state in a cycle. Transcript unit note: intermediate results are written as 350/450 kJ/kg though $c_v,c_p$ are given in J/kg-K.
