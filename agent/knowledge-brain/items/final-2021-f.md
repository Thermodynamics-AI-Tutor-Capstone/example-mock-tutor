## Problem

Given data: Gas turbine Brayton cycle — inlet temperature 300 K; inlet pressure 0.1 MPa; compressor pressure ratio 25; compressor isentropic efficiency 0.9; maximum temperature 1600 K; turbine isentropic efficiency 1; mass flow 15 kg/s; air properties $c_p=1001\ \mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$, $\gamma=1.4$. Refrigerator vapor-compression cycle — pressure at compressor inlet 0.28 MPa; quality at compressor inlet 1; pressure at compressor outlet 0.9 MPa; compressor isentropic efficiency 1; quality after the condenser 0. (f) Plot the gas turbine cycle on a P-V diagram, indicating the pressures on the y-axis. (6 points)

## What it tests

- Students can sketch and label the states and processes of an air-standard Brayton cycle on a $P$-$\mathcal{V}$ diagram with correct pressure levels.

## Where students go wrong

- Placing states 2 and 3 at different pressure levels even though the combustor is isobaric.
- Placing states 4 and 1 at different pressure levels even though heat rejection is isobaric.
- Mislabelling the compressor and turbine paths or omitting process-direction arrows.
- Not indicating the two pressure levels 0.1 MPa and 2.5 MPa.

## How it is graded

Solution awards +1 for each correctly placed state point, +1 for the overall shape of the cycle, and +1 for the pressure levels.
