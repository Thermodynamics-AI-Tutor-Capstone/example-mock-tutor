# Exam 1 (Summer 2022), problem Conflict I.1: Equal-volume equal-pressure tanks of neon and krypton

## Problem

There are two tanks with the same volume and pressure – one with 10 moles of neon (MW=20.2 kg/kmol) and one with 10 moles of krypton (MW=83.8 kg/kmol).

a) Which tank has a higher temperature and why?

b) Which one has a higher mass and why?

## What it tests

- Students can apply the ideal gas law to compare temperatures when P, V, and N are equal.
- Students can relate mass to molecular weight and number of moles.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Neon and krypton are noble gases, so they behave as ideal gases.
- $P$, $\mathcal{V}$, and $N$ are the same for both tanks.
- $R_u$ is a universal constant.

## Where students go wrong

- Comparing temperatures using molecular weight instead of the ideal gas law.
- Thinking the heavier gas must have a higher temperature.
- Using molar mass to compare temperature.
- Forgetting to multiply molecular weight by number of moles when comparing mass.

## How it is graded

Ideal-gas assumption 1 pt; $P\mathcal{V}=NR_uT$ 2 pts; recognizing equal $P,\mathcal{V},N$ 1 pt; temperature answer 2 pts; mass equation 2 pts; mass comparison 2 pts.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Temperature comparison: $T_{Ne}=T_{Kr}$
- Mass comparison: $M_{Kr}>M_{Ne}$
