# Final exam (Summer 2023), problem 2: Power cycle process matching and thermal efficiency

## Problem

All four-step power cycles have the same basic construction. Match the process to the step: Process 1-2, Process 2-3, Process 3-4, Process 4-1 to Heat addition, Compression, Expansion, Heat rejection. What is the performance metric used for all power cycles (provide an equation) and how do each of these steps relate to that metric?

## What it tests

- Students can match the four basic steps of a power cycle to the process numbers and express the thermal efficiency in terms of net work and heat addition.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- All four-step power cycles have the same basic construction.
- Net work is the sum of work in and work out.

## Where students go wrong

- Matching Process 2-3 to expansion or Process 1-2 to heat addition.
- Omitting the contribution of compression to W_in.
- Forgetting that expansion, and sometimes heat addition, can provide W_out.
- Writing an efficiency based on heat rejected instead of heat input.

## How it is graded

Solutions award +2 for the correct matching, +1 for the efficiency equation, +1 for W_in from compression, +1 for W_out from expansion/sometimes heat addition, and +1 for Q_in from heat addition.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Process 1-2: Compression
- Process 2-3: Heat addition
- Process 3-4: Expansion
- Process 4-1: Heat rejection
- $\eta_{th}$: \frac{\dot{W}_{net}}{\dot{Q}_{in}}
