## Problem

Two systems of water start at the critical point. One is cooled isochorically (Process A) and one is cooled isobarically (Process B).

a) Draw these two processes on a T-v diagram, labeling the phases and the vapor dome.

b) Identify the phase of the water after each process.

## What it tests

- Students can draw isochoric and isobaric paths from the critical point on a T-v diagram.
- Students can identify the phase after cooling from the critical point.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Drawing the isobaric process as vertical instead of along the critical isobar.
- Drawing the isochoric process as a curve along the vapor dome.
- Identifying Process A as subcooled liquid.
- Identifying Process B as mixture.
- Not labeling the vapor dome, critical point, or phase regions.

## How it is graded

Diagram points for critical point, dome, phase labels, constant-v line, and critical isobar path; final phases 2 pts each.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Process A final phase: mixture
- Process B final phase: subcooled liquid
