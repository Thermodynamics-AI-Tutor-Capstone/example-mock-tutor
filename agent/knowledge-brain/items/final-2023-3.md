## Problem

An ideal gas in a piston-cylinder device undergoes the following cycle: Process 1-2: Isothermal compression; Process 2-3: Isochoric cooling; Process 3-1: Isobaric expansion. Determine what type of cycle this is. Choices: POWER CYCLE, REFRIGERATION CYCLE, INSUFFICIENT INFO. Draw a P-V diagram of the cycle and explain your answer.

## What it tests

- Students can classify a cycle by determining the direction on a P-v diagram and the sign of net cyclic work.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Ideal gas in a piston-cylinder device.
- Process directions are as listed.

## Where students go wrong

- Classifying as a power cycle without checking direction.
- Drawing the cycle clockwise.
- Not recognizing that counterclockwise integration gives net work into the cycle.
- Confusing isochoric and isobaric path shapes.

## How it is graded

Solutions award +4 for the correct multiple-choice answer; the P-v diagram points reward state labels and process paths, plus credit for counterclockwise direction and net work into the cycle.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Cycle type: Refrigeration cycle
