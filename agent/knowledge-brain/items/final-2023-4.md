# Final exam (Summer 2023), problem 4: Reversible vs irreversible Brayton cycle net power

## Problem

Two Brayton cycles have the same inlet conditions (temperature and pressure), compressor pressure ratios, and maximum temperatures. Cycle A is reversible, whereas Cycle B contains a turbine with an isentropic efficiency of 0.85. Which cycle produces more net power? Choices: CYCLE A, CYCLE B. Explain your answer.

## What it tests

- Students can compare net work of reversible and irreversible Brayton cycles using turbine isentropic efficiency.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Same inlet conditions, compressor pressure ratio, and maximum temperature.
- Cycle B turbine has isentropic efficiency 0.85.
- Compressor conditions are the same for both cycles.

## Where students go wrong

- Choosing Cycle B because the real turbine has losses but not tracing the effect on W_net.
- Forgetting that lower turbine work lowers net power.
- Assuming the real cycle produces more net power than the reversible cycle.
- Using heat addition rather than net work for the comparison.

## How it is graded

Solutions award +2 for choosing Cycle A, +3 for noting the real turbine work is less because η_turb < 1, and +3 for explaining that lower turbine work reduces W_net.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- More net power: Cycle A
