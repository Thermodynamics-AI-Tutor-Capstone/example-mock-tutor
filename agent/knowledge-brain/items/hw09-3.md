## Problem

Explain the difference between a heat pump and a refrigerator. Use their performance metrics to help support your argument.

## What it tests

- Students can distinguish the purpose of a heat pump from that of a refrigerator.
- Students can write the correct coefficient of performance formula for each device.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Using $\dot{Q}_H$ as the desired heat transfer for a refrigerator or $\dot{Q}_L$ for a heat pump.
- Treating coefficient of performance as an efficiency that must be less than 1.
- Describing both devices as generic heat movers without identifying the desired thermal reservoir.
- Giving only a verbal distinction without writing the two performance metrics.

## How it is graded

Points reward correctly identifying the goal of a heat pump and its COP, and the goal of a refrigerator and its COP.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Heat pump goal: Move heat to a warm space
- Heat pump COP: $\beta = \frac{\dot{Q}_H}{\dot{W}_{in}}$ dimensionless
- Refrigerator goal: Remove heat from a cold space
- Refrigerator COP: $\beta = \frac{\dot{Q}_L}{\dot{W}_{in}}$ dimensionless
