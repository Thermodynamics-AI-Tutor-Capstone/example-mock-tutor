## Problem

Which of the following expressions is the correct coefficient of performance (COP) for a refrigerator?

- $\dot{Q}_H/\dot{W}_{in}$
- $\dot{Q}_L/\dot{W}_{in}$
- $\dot{W}_{in}/\dot{Q}_H$
- $\dot{W}_{in}/\dot{Q}_L$

Explain why this is the proper definition for COP for a refrigerator.

## What it tests

- Students can identify the correct COP for a refrigerator and explain the benefit/cost definition.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Selecting $\dot{Q}_H/\dot{W}_{in}$, which is the COP of a heat pump rather than a refrigerator.
- Selecting $\dot{W}_{in}/\dot{Q}_L$, which inverts the desired benefit/cost ratio.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Correct expression: \dot{Q}_L/\dot{W}_{in}
