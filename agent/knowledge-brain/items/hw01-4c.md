## Problem

Consider the five scenarios from the previous question and answer: Indicate whether we should use a system or control volume analysis and why. Draw the scenario and then draw the system or control volume. Label the system/control volume, boundary/control surface, and surroundings. Label the energy and/or mass flows in and/or out of the system/control volume. c. Coffee cup scenario from Question 3: coffee in a mug is placed in a microwave for 1 minute and $Q_{in}$ heat is added to the cup.

## What it tests

- Students can distinguish system vs control volume based on constant mass.
- Students can label boundaries, surroundings, and energy flows.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- System because the water mass is constant.
- Drawing must label system, boundary, surroundings, and heat added.

## Where students go wrong

- Choosing a control volume because heat crosses the boundary, even though mass does not.
- Not labeling the heat input arrow or the system boundary.
- Not labeling surroundings.
