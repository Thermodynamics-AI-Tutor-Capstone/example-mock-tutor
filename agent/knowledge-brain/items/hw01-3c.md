# Homework 1 (Summer 2023), problem 3c: Microwave coffee energy conservation

## Problem

Consider these five conservation scenarios and provide: indicate whether it is appropriate to use a finite-time or instantaneous conservation formulation; write the form of the conservation equation, cancelling any terms not required and explaining why they can be neglected. c. Just as you're about to make more coffee, you find coffee still in the pot from the morning – bonus! You put it in a mug and put it in the microwave for 1 minute, where $Q_{in}$ heat is added to the cup. Write a conservation of energy statement for the coffee in this scenario.

## What it tests

- Students can choose finite-time vs instantaneous conservation formulations based on a finite duration.
- Students can simplify an energy conservation equation by canceling absent terms.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Finite-time formulation because the process has a 1-minute duration.
- No energy generation.
- No energy leaving the coffee except the specified heat added.

## Where students go wrong

- Using instantaneous even though the process has a finite 1-minute duration.
- Including an energy generation term.
- Including a heat loss term not present in the scenario statement.

## How it is graded

+2 for finite time; +2 for generic conservation equation; +2 for final simplified equation.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- formulation: finite time
- conservation of energy equation: \Delta E_{H_2O} = Q_{in}
