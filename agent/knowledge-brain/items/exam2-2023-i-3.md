# Exam 2 (Summer 2023), problem I.3: Sign of compressor power and entropy change

## Problem

A steadily flowing compressor is used to increase the pressure of a fluid and it has an isentropic efficiency of 0.85.

a) What is the sign of the power necessary to run the compressor?

POSITIVE
NEGATIVE
EQUAL TO ZERO

b) Explain your response to the previous question – use an equation to support your answer.

c) What is the sign of the change in entropy of the fluid as it goes through the compressor?

POSITIVE
NEGATIVE
EQUAL TO ZERO

d) Explain your response to the previous question – use an equation to support your answer.

## What it tests

- Students can apply the steady-flow energy equation to a compressor.
- Students can determine the sign of work using the enthalpy change.
- Students can relate isentropic efficiency less than 1 to irreversibility and entropy generation.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Steady flow.
- Negligible kinetic and potential energy changes.
- Adiabatic compressor for the energy-balance explanation.
- For entropy sign: adiabatic and irreversible because \(\eta_{isen}<1\).

## Where students go wrong

- Writing \(\dot{W}=\dot{m}(h_2-h_1)\) without the negative sign from the steady-flow energy balance, and thus getting a positive compressor power.
- Thinking that power input to a device must be positive in all sign conventions, without using the course sign convention in the first-law equation.
- Assuming an adiabatic compressor must be isentropic, so \(\Delta s=0\).
- Using \(\Delta s = \int \frac{\delta Q}{T}\) with \(\delta Q=0\) and forgetting that irreversible entropy generation \(\Phi>0\).

## How it is graded

Part (a): correct sign. Part (b): points for steady-flow energy equation, setting \(\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0\) and \(\dot{Q}=0\), deriving \(\dot{W}=-\dot{m}(h_2-h_1)\), and concluding \(\dot{W}<0\). Part (c): correct sign. Part (d): points for using \(\Delta s=\int \delta Q/T + \Phi\), noting adiabatic means the heat term is zero, and noting irreversibility gives \(\Delta s>0\).

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Sign of power necessary to run the compressor: NEGATIVE
- Sign of change in entropy of the fluid through the compressor: POSITIVE
