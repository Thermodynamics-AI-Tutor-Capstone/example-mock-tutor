## Problem

Draw an isochoric process from $800\ \text{K}$ to $400\ \text{K}$ on a T-V diagram. Label your axes, the two states, and the path between them.

## What it tests

- Students can sketch an isochoric process on a T-V diagram and label axes, states, and path direction.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Drawing a horizontal line instead of vertical because isochoric means constant volume.
- Placing state 1 at the lower temperature and state 2 at the higher temperature.
- Forgetting the arrow or pointing it upward.
- Not labeling axes or states.

## How it is graded

Credit for correctly labeled axes, two states, and the vertical path with direction.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- T-V diagram: Vertical line at constant $\mathcal{V}$ from state 1 at $T = 800\ \text{K}$ to state 2 at $T = 400\ \text{K}$, arrow pointing downward; axes labeled $T$ (K) and $\mathcal{V}$ (m$^3$); horizontal dashed lines at $T = 800\ \text{K}$ and $400\ \text{K}$.
