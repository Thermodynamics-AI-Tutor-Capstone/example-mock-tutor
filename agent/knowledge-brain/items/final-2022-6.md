## Problem

Two vapor compression cycles have the same compressor inlet condition (temperature and pressure), the same compressor pressure ratio, and the same heat extracted from the cold space. Cycle A is reversible, whereas Cycle B contains a compressor with an isentropic efficiency of 0.85. Which cycle rejects more heat to the warm space?

**CYCLE A**

**CYCLE B**

Explain your answer to the previous question.

## What it tests

- Students can use the first law to compare heat rejection when compressor irreversibility changes the required work input.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Same compressor inlet condition, pressure ratio, and same heat extracted from the cold space; only the compressor isentropic efficiency differs.

## Where students go wrong

- Forgetting that $\dot{Q}_L$ is the same in both cycles.
- Ignoring that the less efficient compressor requires more work input, so more heat is rejected.
- Using the heat-pump COP instead of the refrigerator energy balance $\dot{Q}_H = \dot{Q}_L + \dot{W}_{in}$.

## How it is graded

Choice worth 2 pts; explanation awarded 2 pts for writing $\dot{Q}_H = \dot{Q}_L + \dot{W}_{in}$, 2 pts for comparing $\dot{W}_{in,A}<\dot{W}_{in,B}$, and 2 pts for concluding $\dot{Q}_{H,B}>\dot{Q}_{H,A}$.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Correct choice: CYCLE B
