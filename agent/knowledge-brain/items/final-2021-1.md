## Problem

Can the coefficient of performance of a heat pump ever be greater than one? Answer YES or NO, and explain your response. (4 points)

## What it tests

- Students can use $\beta = Q_H/W_{in}$ and the first law to explain why a heat pump COP can exceed one.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Heat pump is a cyclic device.
- Heat absorbed from the cold reservoir is nonzero ($Q_L \neq 0$).

## Where students go wrong

- Choosing NO because COP is treated as an efficiency limited to 1 (misc:m17-cop-treated-as-an-efficiency).
- Forgetting that $Q_H = W_{in} + Q_L$ by the first law.
- Assuming $Q_L = 0$ for a heat pump.

## How it is graded

+2 for correct choice YES; +2 for explanation using $\beta = Q_H/W_{in}$ and the first-law energy balance.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Correct choice: YES
