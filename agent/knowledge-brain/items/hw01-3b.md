# Homework 1 (Summer 2023), problem 3b: Mr. Coffee water energy conservation

## Problem

Consider these five conservation scenarios and provide: indicate whether it is appropriate to use a finite-time or instantaneous conservation formulation; write the form of the conservation equation, cancelling any terms not required and explaining why they can be neglected. b. You want to make coffee using a Mr. Coffee (classic), which heats the water in the well at a rate of $\dot{Q}_{heat}$, but the coffee machine loses heat at a rate of $\dot{Q}_{loss}$. Write a conservation of energy statement for the water in this scenario.

## What it tests

- Students can choose finite-time vs instantaneous conservation formulations based on rates.
- Students can simplify an energy conservation equation by canceling absent terms.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Instantaneous formulation because the scenario gives heating and loss rates.
- No energy generation.
- No work or mass flow terms for the water in the well.

## Where students go wrong

- Using finite-time even though the scenario gives rates.
- Omitting the heat loss term.
- Including an energy generation term.

## How it is graded

+2 for instantaneous; +2 for generic conservation equation; +2 for final simplified equation.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- formulation: instantaneous
- conservation of energy equation: \frac{dE_{water}}{dt} = \dot{Q}_{heat} - \dot{Q}_{loss}
