## Problem

Draw an isothermal process from $3\ \text{MPa}$ to $1\ \text{MPa}$ on a P-T diagram. Label your axes, the two states, and the path between them.

## What it tests

- Students can sketch an isothermal process on a P-T diagram and label axes, states, and path direction.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Drawing a horizontal line instead of vertical because isothermal on P-T means constant temperature.
- Placing state 1 at the lower pressure and state 2 at the higher pressure.
- Forgetting the arrow or pointing it upward.
- Not labeling axes or states.

## How it is graded

Credit for correctly labeled axes, two states, and the vertical path with direction.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- P-T diagram: Vertical line at constant T from state 1 at $P = 3\ \text{MPa}$ to state 2 at $P = 1\ \text{MPa}$, arrow pointing downward; axes labeled $P$ and $T$; horizontal dashed lines at the two pressure values.
