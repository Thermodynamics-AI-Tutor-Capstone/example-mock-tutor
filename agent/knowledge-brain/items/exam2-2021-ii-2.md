# Exam 2 (Summer 2021), problem II.2: Reversible Carnot cycle efficiency, heat transfer, and entropy changes

## Problem

A reversible Carnot cycle operates between 1200 K and 300 K with the following four steps: Process 1-2: isentropic compression; Process 2-3: isothermal heat addition; Process 3-4: isentropic expansion; Process 4-1: isothermal heat rejection. (a) What is the thermal efficiency of this cycle? (b) If 10 kJ of heat is rejected from the cycle in Process 4-1, how much heat was added in Process 2-3 in kJ? (c) What is the change in entropy of the working fluid for each process in the cycle in J/K?

## What it tests

- Students can calculate reversible Carnot-cycle efficiency from reservoir temperatures.
- Students can relate heat addition and rejection through the first law and thermal efficiency.
- Students can compute entropy changes for isentropic and isothermal reversible processes.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- The cycle is a reversible Carnot cycle, so $\eta_{th} = 1 - T_L/T_H$.
- Processes 1-2 and 3-4 are isentropic, so their entropy changes are zero.
- Heat transfer in processes 2-3 and 4-1 occurs at constant temperature; use $\Delta S = \int (\delta Q/T)_{rev}$.

## Where students go wrong

- Using a Carnot formula with $T_L/T_H$ reversed, giving an efficiency greater than 1 or negative work.
- Using the rejected 10 kJ as the heat input instead of the heat rejected.
- Forgetting that the heat-rejection entropy change is negative.
- Reporting answers in kJ/K instead of J/K, for example writing 0.0333 kJ/K.
- Forgetting that isentropic compression and expansion steps have zero entropy change.
- Using non-Carnot efficiency relations even though the cycle is stated reversible.

## How it is graded

Part (a): 6 pts for Carnot formula, temperatures, and efficiency. Part (b): 6 pts for the first-law and efficiency relation. Part (c): 8 pts total; isentropic processes earn 1 pt each and the two isothermal heat-transfer entropy changes earn 2 pts each, with sign.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- $\eta_{th}$: 0.75
- ${}_2Q_3$: 40 kJ
- $\Delta S_{1-2}$: 0 J/K
- $\Delta S_{2-3}$: 33.3 J/K
- $\Delta S_{3-4}$: 0 J/K
- $\Delta S_{4-1}$: -33.3 J/K
