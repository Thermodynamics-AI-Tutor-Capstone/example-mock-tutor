## Problem

Draw an isobaric process from $3\ \text{m}^3$ to $2\ \text{m}^3$ on a P-V diagram. Label your axes, the two states, and the path between them.

## What it tests

- Students can sketch an isobaric process on a P-V diagram and label axes, states, and path direction.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Drawing a vertical line instead of horizontal because isobaric means constant pressure.
- Placing state 1 at the smaller volume and state 2 at the larger volume.
- Forgetting the arrow or pointing it to the right.
- Not labeling axes or states.

## How it is graded

Credit for correctly labeled axes, two states, and the horizontal path with direction.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- P-V diagram: Horizontal line at constant P from state 1 at $\mathcal{V} = 3\ \text{m}^3$ to state 2 at $\mathcal{V} = 2\ \text{m}^3$, arrow pointing to the left; axes labeled $P$ (kPa) and $\mathcal{V}$ (m$^3$); dashed vertical lines at $\mathcal{V} = 2\ \text{m}^3$ and $3\ \text{m}^3$.
