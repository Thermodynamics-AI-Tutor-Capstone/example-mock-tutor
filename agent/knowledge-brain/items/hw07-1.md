# Homework 7 (Summer 2023), problem 1: Steady-flow device table

## Problem

Fill in the table regarding steady-flow devices and their operation: Nozzle, Diffuser, Throttle, Heat Exchanger, Compressor, Turbine. For each device give Purpose, Assumptions, and steady-flow energy equation. (p. 1)

## What it tests

- Students can identify the purpose of common steady-flow devices.
- Students can state the assumptions used to reduce the steady-flow energy equation for each device.
- Students can write the reduced steady-flow energy equation for each device.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- Nozzle: $\dot{W}=\dot{Q}=\Delta pe=0$.
- Diffuser: $\dot{W}=\dot{Q}=\Delta pe=0$.
- Throttle: $\dot{W}=\dot{Q}=\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0$.
- Heat exchanger: $\dot{W}=\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0$.
- Compressor: $\dot{Q}=\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0$.
- Turbine: $\dot{Q}=\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0$.

## Where students go wrong

- Using the same sign for compressor work and turbine work.
- Including heat transfer in the nozzle/diffuser or compressor/turbine reduced equations.
- Forgetting kinetic energy change for nozzle/diffuser, or including kinetic/potential energy for throttle.
- Writing the energy equation without mass-flow rate or using specific quantities inconsistently.

## How it is graded

Each device row: +1 purpose, +1 assumptions, +1 steady-flow energy equation; heat exchanger, compressor, and turbine each have an additional +1 for correct sign.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Nozzle: Purpose: speed up flow. Assumptions: $\dot{W}=\dot{Q}=\Delta pe=0$. Steady-flow energy equation: $\Delta h+\Delta ke=0$.
- Diffuser: Purpose: slow down flow. Assumptions: $\dot{W}=\dot{Q}=\Delta pe=0$. Steady-flow energy equation: $\Delta h+\Delta ke=0$.
- Throttle: Purpose: control flow by dropping pressure. Assumptions: $\dot{W}=\dot{Q}=\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0$. Steady-flow energy equation: $\Delta h=0$.
- Heat exchanger: Purpose: exchange heat, sometimes from one fluid to another. Assumptions: $\dot{W}=\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0$. Steady-flow energy equation: $\dot{m}\Delta h=\dot{Q}$.
- Compressor: Purpose: increase enthalpy of fluid by increasing pressure, work in relative. Assumptions: $\dot{Q}=\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0$. Steady-flow energy equation: $\dot{m}\Delta h=-\dot{W}$.
- Turbine: Purpose: extract work by expanding fluid as pressure drops. Assumptions: $\dot{Q}=\Delta ke=\Delta pe=0$. Steady-flow energy equation: $\dot{m}\Delta h=\dot{W}$.
