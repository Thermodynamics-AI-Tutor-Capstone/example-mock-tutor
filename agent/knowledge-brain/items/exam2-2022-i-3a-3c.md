## Problem

Choose the device you would use to achieve each of the design goals below and describe why. Options for each goal: NOZZLE, HEAT EXCHANGER, DIFFUSER, COMPRESSOR, THROTTLE, TURBINE.

a) Decelerate a flow of air before it enters a boiler system.

b) Control the flow of water into a tank.

c) Increase both the temperature and pressure of air before it enters a processing unit.

## What it tests

- Students can match a practical flow or compression task to the correct steady-flow device.
- Students can explain the physical mechanism responsible for the device behavior.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Selecting a nozzle for deceleration, reversing the area-velocity relationship.
- Choosing a valve or pump without recognizing the throttle's pressure-drop flow-control role.
- Choosing a heat exchanger or turbine to increase pressure; neither does pressure-increasing shaft work on the fluid like a compressor.

## How it is graded

Multiple-choice selection plus typed explanation was scored as a combined device choice and reason for each part.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Device for decelerating air: DIFFUSER
- Device for controlling water flow: THROTTLE
- Device for increasing temperature and pressure: COMPRESSOR
