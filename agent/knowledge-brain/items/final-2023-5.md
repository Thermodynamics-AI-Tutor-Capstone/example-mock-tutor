# Final exam (Summer 2023), problem 5: Entropy changes in an irreversible vapor compression cycle

## Problem

A working fluid undergoes a vapor compression cycle where the isentropic efficiency of the compressor is 0.8. What is the change in the entropy of the working fluid over this cycle? Choices: GREATER THAN ZERO, LESS THAN ZERO, EQUAL TO ZERO. What is the change in the entropy of the surroundings over this cycle? Choices: GREATER THAN ZERO, LESS THAN ZERO, EQUAL TO ZERO. Explain your answers.

## What it tests

- Students can determine that entropy is a property so the cyclic change of the working fluid is zero, and apply entropy generation to the surroundings in an irreversible cycle.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- The cycle returns the working fluid to its initial state.
- Compressor isentropic efficiency 0.8 implies irreversibility.
- The surroundings are included in the second-law balance.

## Where students go wrong

- Saying the working fluid entropy increases because the compressor is irreversible.
- Applying 'entropy always increases' to the working fluid only.
- Saying surroundings entropy change is zero for a cycle.
- Not using entropy as a state property.

## How it is graded

Solutions award +2 for each correct multiple-choice answer, +3 for cycle entropy of the working fluid is zero because entropy is a property, and +3 for S_gen > 0 leading to surroundings entropy increase.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- $\Delta S_{working fluid over cycle}$: equal to zero
- $\Delta S_{surroundings over cycle}$: greater than zero
