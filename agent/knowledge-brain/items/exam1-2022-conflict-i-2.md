## Problem

Two systems of water start at the critical point. One is heated isochorically (Process A) and one is heated isobarically (Process B).

a) Draw these two processes on a T-v diagram, labeling the phases and the vapor dome.

b) Identify the phase of the water after each process.

## What it tests

- Students can draw isochoric and isobaric paths from the critical point on a T-v diagram.
- Students can identify the phase after heating from the critical point.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Drawing Process A as a curve changing specific volume.
- Saying Process A ends as saturated mixture.
- Saying Process B ends as subcooled liquid.
- Not labeling the critical point, vapor dome, or phase regions.

## How it is graded

No grading marks shown for this conflict version; diagram and phase identifications would be assessed similarly to the regular version.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Process A final phase: supercritical fluid
- Process B final phase: superheated vapor or supercritical – right on the line
