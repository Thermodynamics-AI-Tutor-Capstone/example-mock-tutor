## Problem

Steam Plant Rankine Cycle data: quality of water before pump x=0; pressure before pump=0.7 MPa; pressure after pump=10 MPa; pump isentropic efficiency=0.85; heat added in boiler=128.753 MW; turbine isentropic efficiency=1; water mass flow=50 kg/s. Vapor Compression Cycle data (ideal compression): R-134a quality at compressor inlet=1; R-134a pressure at compressor inlet=0.2 MPa; R-134a pressure at compressor outlet=1.4 MPa; R-134a quality at condenser outlet=0. g) Plot the Rankine cycle on a T-s diagram, indicating the pressures using isobars.

## What it tests

- Students can sketch a Rankine cycle on T-s coordinates with the saturation dome, labeled states, and pressure isobars.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- The cycle path follows state 1 to 2, 2 to 3, 3 to 4, and 4 to 1.
- Pressure levels are 0.7 MPa and 10 MPa.
- State 1 is saturated liquid, state 4 is inside the dome, state 3 is superheated.

## Where students go wrong

- Drawing the cycle in the wrong direction.
- Placing state 3 inside or on the saturated vapor line when it is superheated.
- Not labeling the 0.7 MPa and 10 MPa isobars.
- Omitting the saturation dome.
- Drawing the pump path incorrectly as a large entropy increase.

## How it is graded

Solutions award credit for the dome, the isobars, the four states, and each of the four process paths.
