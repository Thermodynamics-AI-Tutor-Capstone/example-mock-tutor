# Homework 9 (Summer 2023), problem 4: Refrigerator diagram, maximum COP, and actual heat flows

## Problem

You find specifications for a refrigerator that say the following: Power requirement $1000\ \mathrm{W}$; $T_L = 0^\circ\mathrm{C}$; $T_H = 20^\circ\mathrm{C}$. (a) Draw a diagram for this refrigerator showing the heat and work flows as well as the two temperature reservoirs. (b) What is the maximum coefficient of performance that you could achieve with this refrigerator? (c) You run some tests and find that the coefficient of performance is actually $7$. Determine the heat in and heat rejected from this refrigerator.

## What it tests

- Students can draw a refrigerator energy flow diagram with heat, work, and temperature reservoirs.
- Students can compute the maximum Carnot COP for a refrigerator.
- Students can use actual COP and an energy balance to find the cold-space heat flow and the heat rejected to the warm space.

## Assumptions the solution expects

- steady state
- $T_L = 0^\circ\mathrm{C} = 273.15\ \mathrm{K}$
- $T_H = 20^\circ\mathrm{C} = 293.15\ \mathrm{K}$

## Where students go wrong

- Using Celsius temperatures directly in $\beta_{max}$ and obtaining $\beta_{max}=0$ or $20/0$.
- Using $\beta = \dot{Q}_H/\dot{W}_{in}$ in part (c) for a refrigerator; the refrigerator COP uses $\dot{Q}_L$.
- Energy balance error such as $\dot{Q}_H = \dot{Q}_L$ or $\dot{Q}_H = \dot{Q}_L - \dot{W}_{in}$ instead of $\dot{Q}_H = \dot{Q}_L + \dot{W}_{in}$.
- Missing labels on the diagram for $\dot{Q}_H$, $\dot{Q}_L$, $\dot{W}_{in}$, $T_H$, and $T_L$ or drawing arrows in the wrong direction.
- Ignoring the given actual COP and instead using the maximum COP to find the heat flows.
- Forgetting to convert Celsius to Kelvin before computing the Carnot COP.

## How it is graded

Points reward temperature conversion, correctly labeled diagram elements and arrows, Carnot COP expression and value, actual COP setup, and the two heat-transfer values.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Maximum coefficient of performance: 13.658 dimensionless
- Heat removed from the cold space: 7000 W
- Heat rejected to the warm space: 8000 W
