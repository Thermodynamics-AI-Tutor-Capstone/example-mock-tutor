## Problem

Draw an isochoric process from $200\ \text{kPa}$ to $500\ \text{kPa}$ on a P-V diagram. Label your axes, the two states, and the path between them.

## What it tests

- Students can sketch an isochoric process on a P-V diagram and label axes, states, and path direction.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Drawing a horizontal line instead of vertical because isochoric means constant volume.
- Placing state 1 at the higher pressure and state 2 at the lower pressure.
- Forgetting the arrow or pointing it downward.
- Not labeling axes or states.

## How it is graded

Credit for correctly labeled axes, two states, and the vertical path with direction.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- P-V diagram: Vertical line at constant $\mathcal{V}$ from state 1 at $P = 200\ \text{kPa}$ to state 2 at $P = 500\ \text{kPa}$, arrow pointing upward; axes labeled $P$ (kPa) and $\mathcal{V}$ (m$^3$); horizontal dashed lines at $P = 200\ \text{kPa}$ and $500\ \text{kPa}$.
