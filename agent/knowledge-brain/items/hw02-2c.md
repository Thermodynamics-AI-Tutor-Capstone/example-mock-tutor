## Problem

Draw an isobaric process from $1200\ \text{K}$ to $800\ \text{K}$ on a P-T diagram. Label your axes, the two states, and the path between them.

## What it tests

- Students can sketch an isobaric process on a P-T diagram and label axes, states, and path direction.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Drawing a vertical line instead of horizontal because isobaric means constant pressure.
- Placing state 1 at the lower temperature and state 2 at the higher temperature.
- Forgetting the arrow or pointing it to the right.
- Not labeling axes or states.

## How it is graded

Credit for correctly labeled axes, two states, and the horizontal path with direction.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- P-T diagram: Horizontal line at constant P from state 1 at $T = 1200\ \text{K}$ to state 2 at $T = 800\ \text{K}$, arrow pointing to the left; axes labeled $P$ (kPa) and $T$ (K); dashed vertical lines at $T = 800\ \text{K}$ and $1200\ \text{K}$.
