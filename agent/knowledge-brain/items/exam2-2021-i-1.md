## Problem

Can the thermal efficiency of a heat engine ever reach one? Answer YES or NO and explain your response. Use $\eta_{th} = \frac{\dot{W}_{net}}{\dot{Q}_{in}}$ in your explanation.

## What it tests

- Students can apply the Kelvin-Planck statement of the second law to a heat engine and use the thermal-efficiency definition to justify why $\eta_{th}<1$.

## Assumptions the solution expects



## Where students go wrong

- Saying YES because an ideal/reversible engine is imagined to have $\eta_{th}=1$.
- Confusing coefficient of performance with thermal efficiency.
- Stating the second law without connecting it to $\dot{W}_{net} < \dot{Q}_{in}$, so $\eta_{th}<1$.

## How it is graded

Correct multiple-choice answer earns 2 pts; explanation using Kelvin-Planck and $\eta_{th}<1$ earns 3 pts.

## Final answers (hidden — for checking only)

- Can thermal efficiency reach one?: NO
