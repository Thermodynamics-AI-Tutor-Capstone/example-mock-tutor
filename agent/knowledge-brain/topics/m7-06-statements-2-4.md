# M7.6 — Statements 2–4

## What this lecture covers

This short deck moves from a recap of Statement 1 to Statements 2–4 of the Second Law: Clausius's direction statement for spontaneous heat flow, the entropy statement for a system and its surroundings, and the maximum-entropy condition for equilibrium.

## Key ideas

- Statement 1 is recapped on the opening slide with handwritten cycle relations (p. 2).
- Statement 2, Clausius's original statement: heat flows spontaneously from $T_H$ to $T_L$, and it requires work to have heat flow from $T_L$ to $T_H$ (p. 4).
- Spontaneous processes have a preferred direction and move toward equilibrium (p. 4).
- The preferred direction of spontaneous heat flow is the basis for refrigeration (p. 4).
- Statement 3, the Clausius entropy statement: the entropy of a system and its surroundings increases, or in the limit of a reversible process, stays constant (p. 5).
- Statement 4: equilibrium is achieved when entropy is maximized, for a simple system at constant $u$ and $v$ (p. 6).

## Notation used

- $S$: entropy; the slide lists $S \sim [\text{J/K}]$ and $S = [\text{J/kg-K}]$ (p. 5).
- $\bar{S}$: molar entropy; the slide lists $\bar{S} = [\text{J/mol-K}]$ (p. 5).
- $u$: internal energy, with units $[\text{J/kg}]$ (p. 6).
- $v$: mass-specific volume, with units $[\text{m}^3/\text{kg}]$ (p. 6).
