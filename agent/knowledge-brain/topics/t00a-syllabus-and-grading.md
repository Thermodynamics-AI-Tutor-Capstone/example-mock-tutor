# Grading and policies (Summer 2023 section)

**Weights:** homework 25 % (lowest homework dropped), explained examples 15 %, quizzes 20 %
(two lowest dropped), exams 40 %.

**Scale:** A 93, A− 90, B+ 87, B 83, B− 80, C+ 77, C 70, D 60.

**Contesting a grade:** email a formal request — the question, the original answer and a
paragraph explaining why — no earlier than 24 hours after the work is returned. Verbal requests
are not considered. Obvious grading mistakes are corrected immediately; subjective ones are
reconsidered at the end of the semester if they could change the final grade.

**Late drops:** before Exam 1, WN; after Exam 1, WP with a score ≥ 60 % or WF below.
