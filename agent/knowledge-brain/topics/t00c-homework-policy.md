# Assignment types (Summer 2023 section)

- **Homework** — weekly, due Fridays except exam weeks. Do as much as you can alone first, then
  office hours, the discussion board or peers. "Exam questions will closely resemble homework
  questions." Show all work: partial credit needs it.
- **Quizzes** — short, open-book, open-note Canvas check-ins on the videos and book. Three
  attempts each; the score is the **average** of the attempts. Questions may change between
  attempts but test the same concepts.
- **Explained examples (EE)** — three per student over the term, done in pairs, based on Brent
  and Felder's finding that explaining worked examples to each other builds deeper understanding.
  Part 1: record a Zoom call explaining the example step by step, switching presenters (graded
  for completion). Part 2 (next week): watch Dr. O'Connor's video of the same example and write a
  reflection comparing the two explanations.
- **Exams** — individual, open book, open note, 1.5 h proctored on Zoom plus 15 min to upload
  written work; concept questions and worked problems; practice exams with solutions posted
  beforehand.

**Late work:** −15 % in the first 24 hours, −50 % after that. Contact the instructor before the
due date for University-approved reasons. No make-up quizzes or exams except as University
policy requires.
