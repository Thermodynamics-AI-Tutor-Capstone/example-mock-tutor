# Logistics (Summer 2023 section)

- **Instructor:** Dr. Jacqueline O'Connor, Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering. The
  course design acknowledges Dr. Andrea Gregg (online pedagogy).
- **Format:** fully online and asynchronous; no required meetings except the three exams.
  Lectures are short videos with built-in activities; the same material is in the annotated
  slides and the textbook.
- **Office hours:** weekly, times chosen by a class poll in week 1.
- **Communication:** by PSU email, not Canvas messages; put "ME 300" and the topic in the subject;
  professional tone with a salutation and closing; replies within one business day.

A student in another section should use their own instructor's contact details and office hours
from Canvas.
