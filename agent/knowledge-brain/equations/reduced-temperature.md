# Reduced temperature

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
T_R = \frac{T}{T_c}
$$

Reduced temperature is the absolute temperature divided by the critical temperature T_c of the substance. It is a dimensionless state parameter used to enter generalized property charts, such as compressibility factor charts, independent of the particular substance. Use the same absolute temperature units for numerator and denominator.
