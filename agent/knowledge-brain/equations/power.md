# Power

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
$\dot W = \vec{F}\cdot\vec{V}$
$$

Power $\dot W$ is the time rate of work transfer. It is the dot product of instantaneous force and velocity at the point where the force acts. For a piston-cylinder boundary, this can be specialized to pressure force times boundary velocity.
