# Displacement volume

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
d=\mathcal{V}_1-\mathcal{V}_2
$$

This gives the swept volume of a reciprocating engine as the difference between the largest cylinder volume at bottom dead center and the smallest volume at top dead center. It is a purely geometric relation and is often given or asked for in engine problems. The compression ratio and displacement together can determine the cylinder volumes. Students sometimes confuse displacement with clearance volume or use it in the ideal-gas law without accounting for mass. Use this only for piston-cylinder geometry.
