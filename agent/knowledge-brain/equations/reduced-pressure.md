# Reduced pressure

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
P_R = \frac{P}{P_c}
$$

Reduced pressure is the pressure divided by the critical pressure P_c. Like reduced temperature, it is dimensionless and used with generalized compressibility charts. Always use absolute pressure, not gauge pressure, and use the same units for numerator and denominator. Combined with T_R, it determines Z for a real gas in the generalized compressibility method.
