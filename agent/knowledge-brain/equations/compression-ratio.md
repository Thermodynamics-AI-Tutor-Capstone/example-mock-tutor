# Compression ratio

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
r=\frac{\mathcal{V}_1}{\mathcal{V}_2}
$$

The compression ratio is the ratio of the largest cylinder volume to the smallest cylinder volume. In reciprocating engines, it is a geometric parameter that strongly influences thermal efficiency. The same definition can be written with specific volumes v_1/v_2. Higher compression ratio gives higher ideal Otto and Diesel efficiency. Students sometimes confuse compression ratio with pressure ratio or cutoff ratio. Use this definition when the cycle diagram labels volumes at bottom dead center and top dead center. It is not an energy or property equation itself.
