# Simple compressible substance relation

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
$v = f(P,T)$
$$

For a simple compressible substance, two independent intensive properties fix the state. Choosing pressure and temperature then determines specific volume. This underlies property tables and the ideal-gas model. It is a general property relation, not an energy balance.
