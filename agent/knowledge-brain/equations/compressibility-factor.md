# Compressibility factor

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
Z = \frac{Pv}{RT}
$$

The compressibility factor is the dimensionless ratio of the actual Pv product to the ideal-gas value RT. The molar-specific form is Z = P v-bar / (R_u T). For an ideal gas Z = 1; values above or below 1 indicate real-gas effects. Use Z to decide whether the ideal-gas model is reasonable or to correct calculations. Reduced pressure and reduced temperature are used with generalized compressibility charts.
