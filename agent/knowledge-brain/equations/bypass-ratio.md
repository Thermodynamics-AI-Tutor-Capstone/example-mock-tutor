# Bypass ratio

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
\text{bypass ratio}=\frac{\dot{m}_{\mathrm{bypass}}}{\dot{m}_{\mathrm{core}}}
$$

This defines the bypass ratio in a turbofan engine. The numerator is the mass flow through the bypass duct, and the denominator is the mass flow through the core where combustion occurs. A high bypass ratio means most of the air goes around the core, which improves propulsive efficiency for transport aircraft. The slide annotates a value of 12. This is simply a flow-split parameter, not an energy or property relation. Use it when given or asked to find stream mass flow rates.
