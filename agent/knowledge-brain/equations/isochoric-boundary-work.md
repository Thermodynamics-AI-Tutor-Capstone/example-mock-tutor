# Isochoric boundary work

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
${}_1W_2 = 0$
$$

Boundary work requires a volume change. In a rigid tank or other constant-volume system, $d\mathcal{V}=0$, so the integral $\int P\,d\mathcal{V}$ is zero regardless of pressure changes. This does not mean no energy transfer; heat can still cross the boundary and change internal energy.
