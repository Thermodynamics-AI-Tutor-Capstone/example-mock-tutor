# Internal energy decomposition

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
U = U_{\mathrm{trans}} + U_{\mathrm{rot}} + U_{\mathrm{vib}}
$$

Total internal energy is the sum of molecular-level translational, rotational, and vibrational contributions for species that possess those modes. This decomposition explains that internal energy is stored at the molecular scale and is not the same as temperature. Use it when interpreting how energy is distributed within a gas or when explaining why different molecules have different specific heats. The equation $U = U_{\mathrm{trans}} + U_{\mathrm{rot}} + U_{\mathrm{vib}}$ is conceptual; in practical property tables you will use $u$ or $U$ directly rather than computing these terms separately.
