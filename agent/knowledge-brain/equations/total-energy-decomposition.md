# Total energy decomposition

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
$E = E_{\mathrm{bulk}} + U$
$$

Total energy $E$ is separated into $E_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ and internal energy $U$. The bulk part is the mechanical energy of the whole system moving or elevated in a gravity field; internal energy is the energy stored at the molecular scale. The course uses this decomposition when kinetic and potential energy changes may be non-negligible, so they are not automatically dropped. A common error is to call every stored energy internal energy or to neglect $E_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ when the system moves as a rigid body.
