# Differential work

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
$\delta W = \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{s}$
$$

Work is an inexact differential, written $\delta W$ rather than $dW$ because it depends on the path, not only the end states. The dot product picks out the component of force in the direction of the displacement. For a finite process, the total work is the path integral of this quantity.
