# Adiabatic closed-system energy balance

> Drafted by AI (DeepSeek, from transcripts of the course files; checked by Claude) — not reviewed by an instructor. Handwriting in the source was occasionally misread; the cited pages are the authority.

$$
$\Delta E = -{}_1W_2$
$$

Setting heat transfer to zero in the closed-system energy balance leaves $\Delta E = -{}_1W_2$. With the course sign convention, if work is done by the system, ${}_1W_2>0$ and stored energy decreases. If work is done on the system, ${}_1W_2<0$ and stored energy increases.
