Kelvin AI is a capstone prototype. It stores your account email, the profile fields above, your chats, and notes Kelvin makes about what you've learned (deleted along with the chat they came from). The capstone team can see all of them. Messages you send, and any course material Kelvin reads to answer you, are sent to DeepSeek's API to generate replies. While "Use Jev" is on, your messages and Kelvin's replies are also sent to TypeSafe's Jev model through OpenRouter.
