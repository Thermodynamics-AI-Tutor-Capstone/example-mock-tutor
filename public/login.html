About this study. Kelvin is a Penn State capstone prototype and part of a research study on AI in engineering education: we're trying to build a tutor that's better than ChatGPT and Claude at teaching students. Your chats are saved and the capstone research team can read them, and your messages are sent to AI services (DeepSeek, and TypeSafe's Jev) to write Kelvin's replies. Kelvin never asks for your real name: please don't type your name, PSU ID or other personal details into your chats.
